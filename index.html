Shitbox Rally

v0.6.2 released

A rally racing game about tired old cars on procedurally generated stages, with a PS1/PS2-era look. Built from the physics up in Godot 4 with Jolt: raycast suspension, a slip-ratio/slip-angle tyre model, simulated clutch, gearboxes and differentials, and engine sounds rendered from real torque curves.

Now with a career mode (buy a cheap car, race for money, pay for your own damage), online multiplayer over Steam with lobbies, invites and push-to-talk radio, up to 4-player split-screen, and ten real roads such as the Stelvio Pass and Pikes Peak built from OpenStreetMap and elevation data.

  • 25+ cars with physical specs
  • 14 road surfaces
  • 10 real-world stages
  • 115 GDScript files

WA Commute Crash Risk

Live

Maps every fatal traffic crash in Washington from 2010 to 2024 using NHTSA data and estimates your odds of a crash on your own commute.

Enter two addresses and it routes the drive, grades each alternate route, and flags the deadliest one-mile stretches. Results adjust for road type, local crash history, time of day, weather and driver habits, based on published traffic-safety research.
